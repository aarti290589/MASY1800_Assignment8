
# Assignment 8 - Context Contrast Test Comparison

## Emerging Technology
Generative AI using Large Language Models (LLMs)

## Assessment Date
November 16, 2026

## Tests Conducted

Primary: AI customer service assistant for a mid-sized
US e-commerce retailer.

Contrast 1: AI-assisted clinical documentation for a
large US hospital network.

Contrast 2: AI-powered coding assistant for a
US-based AI-native software startup.

## Finding That Remained Stable

Across the three responses, Generative AI using LLMs
was assessed as commercially available for selected,
bounded assistance tasks.

The responses consistently recognized limitations
in autonomous reliability, factual accuracy,
security, and the need for oversight.

The overall evidence supports supervised assistance
more strongly than unrestricted autonomous
execution of consequential activities.

## Findings That Appropriately Changed

Primary:
The assessment prioritized conventional chatbots,
deterministic workflow automation, human support,
CRM integration, customer data protection, and
cost per correctly resolved inquiry.

Contrast 1:
The assessment prioritized conventional medical
transcription, structured EHR documentation,
clinician review, clinical accuracy, HIPAA-related
privacy requirements, and patient safety.

Contrast 2:
The assessment prioritized traditional IDE
capabilities, static analysis, automated testing,
human code review, model pricing, software security,
and measured developer productivity.

These differences demonstrate that the agent
adjusted its competitive analysis and monitoring
priorities to the intended application and
organization.

## Weakness Identified

The general_et_finding field did not consistently
maintain the same analytical scope across cases.

The hospital response incorporated detailed
clinical documentation findings into the general
technology assessment, while the customer service
and software development responses emphasized
their respective application domains.

This reduced the comparability of the shared
general technology baseline.

## Revision Approach

The specialist instructions were strengthened to
separate general LLM maturity from application-
specific maturity and organization-specific
management implications.

Detailed clinical research and healthcare
requirements should remain in the analysis but
be positioned under the appropriate context.
